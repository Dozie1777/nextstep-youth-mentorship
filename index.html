Showing 3 resources.

QUICK START · 15 MIN</>

Your very first web page

Turn a blank file into a small introduction to something you care about.

Open the guide ↗
  1. Create a file called index.html.
  2. Add <h1>Hello, world!</h1> and a paragraph about your idea.
  3. Open it in a browser. Change the heading and refresh.
  4. Show a friend and ask what you could make clearer.
REFLECTION · 10 MIN↗

Make your next goal smaller

Trade a big, vague ambition for a next step you can actually take this week.

Open the guide ↗
  1. Write one thing you want to learn.
  2. Choose an action you can finish in 20 minutes.
  3. Pick a day and time to do it.
  4. Afterward, note what worked and choose the next small step.
ACTIVITY · 20 MIN✳

Spot a problem. Start an idea.

Look around your community and discover a small way you could help.

Open the guide ↗
  1. List three everyday challenges you notice.
  2. Choose one you can explore safely with a trusted adult or peer.
  3. Sketch a small improvement and who it would help.
  4. Ask for feedback before deciding what to build.
